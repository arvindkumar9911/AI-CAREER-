# AI Career 🚀

AI Career is an AI-powered career guidance platform designed to help students discover the right career path.

## Our Goal

To help students understand their skills, choose suitable career paths, build required skills, create projects, prepare for interviews, and find career opportunities.

## Key Features

- Personalized career guidance
- Skill-gap analysis
- Personalized learning roadmap
- Project recommendations
- Resume guidance
- Interview preparation
- Internship and job guidance

## Vision

To make career guidance accessible, personalized, and actionable for every student.
